Who We Are

Community Skills Hub is a friendly learning website created to help people improve their digital skills. We understand that technology can sometimes feel difficult, so our aim is to make learning simple, supportive and easy to follow.

Our courses are designed for beginners who want to use computers, the internet and online tools with more confidence. We believe everyone should have the opportunity to learn useful digital skills for study, work and everyday life.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make digital learning accessible, enjoyable and useful for everyone. We want learners to feel confident using technology in their daily lives and future careers.
